FAQs

Everything You Need to Know

Do I need an appointment?

Appointments are recommended, especially for coloring, bridal and longer services. Walk-ins are welcome subject to stylist availability.

How early should I book a bridal appointment?

We recommend booking several weeks in advance so we can plan consultation, trial and final styling comfortably.

Can I choose a specific stylist?

Yes. You can request a preferred stylist in the appointment form. We will confirm availability.

How long does a hair coloring service take?

Timing depends on hair length, thickness, existing color and the chosen technique. Your stylist will provide an estimate during consultation.

Do you provide hair-care recommendations?

Yes. Our stylists can suggest a simple home-care routine based on your hair type and the service performed.

Can I reschedule my appointment?

Yes. Please contact the salon as early as possible so we can move your appointment to another available slot.

Do you offer services for men?

Yes. We offer haircuts, beard styling, facials, head massage and a dedicated men’s grooming package.

Are the prices fixed?

Displayed prices are starting prices. Final pricing can vary with hair length, product usage, service complexity and stylist level.

Do you accept student bookings?

Yes. Students can book regular services and selected student offers when a valid student ID is presented.

Is this website connected to a real booking system?

No. This college project is a front-end demonstration created using HTML5 and CSS3 only. The forms are designed with HTML5 validation but have no server-side processing.

Still Have a Question?

Our team will be happy to help with your service or appointment query.
